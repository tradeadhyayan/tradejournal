Product Goal

Help Indian traders stop losing money due to emotions and build trading discipline.

2. Why Are We Building This? (The Problem)

Most Indian retail traders lose their capital. They don't lose because they don't know technical analysis; they lose because of bad psychology (Revenge trading, FOMO, over-trading, not hitting Stop Loss). Normal journals only show Profit and Loss (PnL), but they don't show why the trader lost that money or how much a specific bad habit cost them in Rupees.

2.1 What is Trade Adhyayan? (The Solution)

Trade Adhyayan is a smart trading journal and discipline tracker. It forces traders to follow rules, tracks their daily mistakes, and shows them exactly how much money (in INR) they are losing because of bad psychology. It acts as a strict digital mentor.

3. Target Audience

3.1 The F&O Option Buyer / Seller (Primary)

  • Profile: Trades BankNifty/Nifty options daily.
  • The Problem: Makes profit in the morning, but overtrades and loses everything by 3:30 PM. Needs strict rules to stop trading.
  • What they want: A daily checklist to follow and an alert that shows "Revenge trading cost you ₹15,000 this week."

3.2 The Equity Intraday/Swing Trader (Secondary)

  • Profile: Trades cash market stocks (Reliance, HDFC, SBI).
  • The Problem: Needs to know which strategy (e.g., Breakout vs. Pullback) is actually making them money.
  • What they want: clear stats like Win Rate and Risk-Reward (RR) ratio for their trades.

5. Technical Details (Web App Architecture & Stack)

The Trade Adhyayan web application is built on a modern, high-performance tech stack designed for speed during active market hours (9:15 AM to 3:30 PM) and strict data security.

  • Frontend Framework: React 18+ built with Vite and TypeScript for fast rendering and type safety.
  • State Management & Fetching: React Query (@tanstack/react-query) is used to cache trade data aggressively on the client side so the app feels instant without waiting for the server every time you click a button.
  • UI & Aesthetics: Tailwind CSS. The design language uses a "Soft premium" style with glassmorphism, subtle blurred background glows, and large, clear typography differentiating between standard text and numbers.
  • Data Visualization: Recharts library is used for building the responsive, smooth-animated Equity Curve area charts on the dashboard.
  • Backend & Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL). Supabase acts as our fully managed Backend-as-a-Service, handling data storage, Row Level Security (RLS) policies, and user management.
  • Authentication: Supabase Auth (Secured Email/Password routes).
  • Hosting & Deployment: Vercel. Code pushed to GitHub automatically triggers a production build pipeline via Vercel for high-uptime, scalable hosting.

6. What We Are NOT Building Right Now (Out of Scope for V1.0)

  • Connecting directly to Zerodha/Upstox/Groww API: Pulling live trades automatically from brokers. The user has to enter trades manually.
  • Options chain/Greeks Data: We are not showing live data for Delta, Theta, or Gamma. We are purely a journal, not a trading terminal.
